Entry for the [make.fun](https://make.fun) bounty **"One Word for Your Wi-Fi"** (sponsor: tuan-muda) — one word that describes Wi-Fi, the funniest AND most accurate one. Free entry, $2 winner-takes-all, judged pairwise on on-subject fit, funniness, truthfulness and freshness of angle. The winning word becomes the sponsor's network name.

## The word

> **OneBar**

## Why OneBar

Everyone has lived it: the bars lie, the video freezes, and the network list always shows the one bar you actually have. As a network name it is a confession and a joke at once — the sponsor names its own Wi-Fi after the signal strength nobody can escape. It is self-deprecating instead of bragging, which is exactly the angle a network name should take: you see it in the list, you laugh, and you still connect.

Candidates, scored 0–5 on fit / funny / true / fresh (see `main.py` for the table):

| word | fit | funny | true | fresh | total |
|------|-----|-------|------|-------|-------|
| **OneBar** | 5 | 5 | 5 | 4 | **19** |
| Deadzone | 4 | 4 | 4 | 3 | 15 |
| Moody | 4 | 4 | 4 | 3 | 15 |
| Clingy | 4 | 4 | 3 | 3 | 14 |
| Buffering | 5 | 2 | 5 | 1 | 13 |
| Reboot | 4 | 3 | 4 | 2 | 13 |
| Throttled | 4 | 2 | 4 | 2 | 12 |

Deliberately avoided: Ghosting, Diva, Gaslighting, ItBurnsWhenIP — already taken by other entries. Buffering lost on freshness: it is the first word everyone thinks of, so it is the last word that wins a pairwise vote.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

It prints the word above, and nothing else. It also writes the same word to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output/entry.txt`), which is what the bounty scores.
